ABOUT THE AUTHORS

DR. EDWARD D. GRAVELY serves as professor of Christian studies at Charleston Southern University and occupies the Ott Chair of Christian Theology. He has taught Greek and New Testament at university and graduate levels since 2002. His research and writing focus on the field of textual criticism.

DR. PETER LINK JR. serves as professor of Christian studies at Charleston Southern University. He has taught biblical Hebrew, Old Testament, and hermeneutics at university and graduate levels since 2012. His research and writing focus on the Pentateuch’s composition, especially in how the various parts of Moses’ book relate to one another.
